# Domain questions — UX/UI design and frontend screens
- Walk the real user flow step by step. Where would a first-time user get stuck or confused?
- Are empty, loading, error and long-content states handled?
- Mobile width (375–390px) and desktop: does anything overflow, overlap or become unreadable?
- Accessibility: text contrast ≥ 4.5:1, touch targets ≥ 44px, keyboard operable, visible focus, labels on inputs and icon buttons, alt text.
- Does the screen follow the project's DESIGN.md tokens, or hard-code colours/sizes?
- Is copy clear and specific (button labels say what they do, errors say how to fix)?
- Is the depth appropriate for the product type (research demo / internal tool / launched service), and are skipped steps justified?
- AI features: are claims linked to sources, uncertainty shown honestly (no uncalibrated % scores), and results editable/undoable, with humans confirming consequential actions?
