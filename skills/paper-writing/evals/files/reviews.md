## Reviewer 1
1. The improvement over CNN-LSTM on MobiAct (0.845 vs 0.832) is within one standard deviation. Please provide a statistical test.
2. The paper claims real-world deployability, but no on-device latency or battery measurement is reported. Please add experiments on an actual smartwatch.
3. Minor: Figure 3 axis labels are too small.

## Reviewer 2
1. Related work misses recent self-supervised HAR methods (e.g., SimCLR-style approaches for IMU data).
2. Why was only a linear head used? An ablation with a fine-tuned encoder would strengthen the paper.
